 ### 1. 含有 $x^2 \pm a^2$ 的积分

41. $$\int \frac{1}{x^2 + a^2} \, dx = \frac{1}{a} \arctan \frac{x}{a} + C$$
42. $$\int \frac{1}{x^2 - a^2} \, dx = \frac{1}{2a} \ln\left| \frac{x-a}{x+a} \right| + C$$
43. $$\int \frac{x}{x^2 \pm a^2} \, dx = \frac{1}{2} \ln|x^2 \pm a^2| + C$$

### 2. 含有 $\sqrt{x^2 \pm a^2}$ 的积分

44. $$\int \frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2 + a^2}} \, dx = \ln\left| x + \sqrt{x^2 + a^2} \right| + C$$
45. $$\int \frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2 - a^2}} \, dx = \ln\left| x + \sqrt{x^2 - a^2} \right| + C$$
46. $$\int \sqrt{x^2 \pm a^2} \, dx = \frac{x}{2} \sqrt{x^2 \pm a^2} \pm \frac{a^2}{2} \ln\left| x + \sqrt{x^2 \pm a^2} \right| + C$$
47. $$\int \frac{\sqrt{x^2 + a^2}}{x} \, dx = \sqrt{x^2 + a^2} - a \ln\left| \frac{a + \sqrt{x^2 + a^2}}{x} \right| + C$$
48. $$\int \frac{\sqrt{x^2 - a^2}}{x} \, dx = \sqrt{x^2 - a^2} - a \arccos \frac{a}{|x|} + C$$

### 3. 含有 $\sqrt{a^2 - x^2}$ 的积分

49. $$\int \frac{1}{\sqrt{a^2 - x^2}} \, dx = \arcsin \frac{x}{a} + C \quad (a > 0)$$
50. $$\int \sqrt{a^2 - x^2} \, dx = \frac{x}{2} \sqrt{a^2 - x^2} + \frac{a^2}{2} \arcsin \frac{x}{a} + C$$
51. $$\int \frac{\sqrt{a^2 - x^2}}{x} \, dx = \sqrt{a^2 - x^2} - a \ln\left| \frac{a + \sqrt{a^2 - x^2}}{x} \right| + C$$
52. $$\int x^2 \sqrt{a^2 - x^2} \, dx = \frac{x}{8} (2x^2 - a^2) \sqrt{a^2 - x^2} + \frac{a^4}{8} \arcsin \frac{x}{a} + C$$
