>[!question]
>$$(1)\lim\limits_{x \to a}\frac{x^x-a^a}{x-a}$$
>$$(2)\lim\limits_{x \to a}\frac{a^x-x^a}{x-a}$$
>$$(3)\lim\limits_{x \to a}\frac{\tan x-\tan a}{x^a-a^a}$$

(1) $$\lim_{x \to a} \frac{x^a - a^a}{x - a} = (x^a)'|_{x=a} = x^a (1 + \ln x)|_{x=a} = a^a (1 + \ln a)$$
这就是一个最标准的形式，完全就是按照极限定义的标准形式来的，是后面的铺垫

(2) $$\lim_{x \to a} \frac{a^x - x^a}{x - a} = \lim_{x \to a} \frac{a^x - a^a}{x - a} - \lim_{x \to a} \frac{x^a - a^a}{x - a} = (a^x)'|_{x=a}  -(x^a)'|_{x=a} = a^a (\ln a - 1)$$
(3) $$\text{原式} = \lim_{x \to a} \frac{\tan x - \tan a}{x - a} \cdot \frac{x - a}{x^a - a^a} =\left. \frac{(\tan x)'}{(x^a)'} \right|_{x=a} =\left.\frac{\sec^2 x}{ax^{a-1}}\right|_{x=a} = \frac{1}{a^a \cos^2 a}$$
